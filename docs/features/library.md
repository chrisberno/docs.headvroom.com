# Library

Your home page. Signing in opens the Library at headvroom.com, with every graph you own as a card. The HeadVroom logo, and the **Library** button on a graph page, bring you back to it.

The older radial Matrix view is still at `/matrix`, by address only.

## Find a graph

- **Search:** press `/` (or click the search box) and type. It matches the start of any word in a graph's name, and capitals and accents do not matter: `zeb` finds *Zebra*, `ebra` finds nothing, `cafe` finds *Café*. Esc clears the box; a second Esc leaves it.
- **Sort:** **Last opened** (the default; graphs you have never opened come last, A to Z), **A–Z**, **Newest**, **Most nodes**.
- **Group:** tick **Group** to split the cards into **Private** and **Public**. It is off each time the page opens.

## Favorites

Click the **star** on a card, or right-click it → **Add to Favorites**. The **Favorites** tab at the top left shows only those, with its count beside **All**. Favorites are saved to your account, so they are the same in every browser and on every device.

There is no **Shared with me** view yet: HeadVroom has no sharing between accounts, and that view comes when sharing does.

## Cards

Each card shows:

- the graph's **name**
- who can see it: **Private**, or **Public** for a graph you published
- its **node count**
- when you **last opened** it (*Never opened* until you do)
- **Stem** on your stem graph

**S** and **L** switch the card size; the two layout buttons switch between a **grid** and a **list**. Size and layout are remembered in this browser only.

## Create a graph

Click **New graph** at the right of the bar. The bar stays in view as you scroll. The new graph opens straight away and is in the Library when you come back.

## The card menu

Right-click a card, or click its **⋮**:

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| Open | Opens the graph |
| Add to / Remove from Favorites | Same as the star |
| Rename | Renames the graph |
| Set as stem | Makes it your stem graph (not shown on the stem graph itself) |
| Move to graph… | Moves the whole graph under a node of another graph: see [Move to graph](move-to-graph) |
| Delete | Deletes the graph with its nodes and links, after asking |
