# Pins

Keep the nodes you return to all the time one click away. Pins stay where they are until you unpin them.

## Pin and unpin

- **Right-click** a node and choose **Pin**, or press the **pin button** at the top of its Info Panel.
- The node joins the **pin row** at the top of the graph, beside the Search/Create box, in the order you pinned.
- Unpin it the same two ways (**Unpin**).
- The row is not shown at all while a graph has no pins. There is no limit; a long row scrolls sideways.

## Use a pin

- Click a pin to go to that node in one click, from anywhere in the graph.
- It counts as a visit: it joins the [trail](./navigation), and **Back** takes you to where you were.
- **Each graph has its own pins**, saved to your account, so they are the same on every device.
- A pinned node that is deleted leaves the row, and comes back if the node is [restored](./history).

## Pins, the trail and Favorites

| | What it holds | Changes when |
|---|---|---|
| **Trail** (bottom of the graph) | The last 20 nodes you visited | Every time you move |
| **Pins** (top of the graph) | Nodes you chose | Only when you pin or unpin |
| **Favorites** (graph library) | Graphs, not nodes | When you mark a graph |
