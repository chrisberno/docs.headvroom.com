# Views

Look at the same graph three ways — **Map**, **Tree** and **Cards** — without losing your place. And, if you like a grid behind your work, turn on the **Dot grid**.

## Switching views

The switcher sits **beside the Search/Create box** at the top of the graph page: **Map · Tree · Cards**. One click switches; there are no keyboard shortcuts for it.

- **Map** is the canvas, and the default.
- **Tree** shows the whole graph as an outline.
- **Cards** shows the children of the node you are on as tiles.

Switching **never loses your place**: the node you are on, the Info Panel, the trail and Back/Forward stay exactly as they were, and the Map is just as you left it when you come back. Search/Create, Back/Forward (`Alt+←` / `Alt+→`) and the trail work the same in every view.

Each graph **remembers its last view in this browser**. A graph you have not switched yet opens on Map.

## Tree

The graph as an outline, starting at the **stem node**. Any node with no parent of its own is listed after the stem.

- **What makes the outline:** parent–child links only. Lateral links do not.
- **Order:** children A to Z; capitals and accents are ignored (*Éclair* sorts between *Beta* and *Gamma*).
- **Several parents:** a node with more than one parent is listed under **each** parent, with a small merge mark.
- **What is open:** the stem's children, plus the branch down to the node you are on, whose row is highlighted.
- **Open or close a row:** click the arrow beside it.
- **Go to a node:** click its row. It joins the trail and Back returns you. The outline stays rooted at the stem.
- **Keys** (once a row has keyboard focus): `↑` / `↓` move, `→` opens a row (or steps into it), `←` closes it (or steps up to its parent), `Enter` goes to the node.

The Tree is for reading and moving around. Rename or re-link nodes in the Map or the Info Panel.

## Cards

The children of the node you are on, **A to Z**, one tile each:

- the node's **icon**,
- its **name**,
- the first **140 characters of its note**, as plain text.

**Click a card** to go to that node; the cards then show *its* children. To go back up, use **Back** (`←` or `Alt+←`) or the **trail**. A node with no children says so.

A child you create with **Search/Create → Create Child** appears as a card at once.

## Dot grid

Open **Settings** (your avatar) → **Appearance** and switch **Dot grid** on. Typing `dot` in the Settings search box finds it.

- **Off by default.** Nothing changes until you switch it on.
- **Where the dots show:** behind the **Map** of every graph, behind the **Matrix** (`/matrix`) and behind your **embedded nodes** on other sites — an embed follows its owner's setting. Tree and Cards have no dots.
- **Applies at once,** with no Save button.
- **Saved to your account,** so it follows you to any browser.
- **Follows your theme:** dark dots on Light, light dots on Dark (Outer Space and Custom get their own shade). The glow behind the node you are on stays either way.
