# Navigation

Get back to anywhere you have been in one click, find any node by typing, and open Help or Settings without leaving the page you are on.

## Search/Create

The **Search/Create** box sits at the top left of the graph, next to the Back and Forward arrows.

- **Focus it:** click it, or press `/`. It never takes the keyboard on its own.
- **What it searches:** the graph you are on, by node **name** and by **notes**.
- **How it matches:** from the start of any word. `alp` finds *Alpha One*; `pha` finds nothing. Capitals and accents do not matter: `cafe` finds *Café*.
- **The list:** name matches first, then notes matches, each A to Z, up to 20 rows. A name used by two nodes shows its parent so you can tell them apart.
- **Keys:** `↑` / `↓` move, `Enter` or a click goes to the node, `Esc` closes the list. The box empties after you go.

### Create a node

Type the new name, then click **Create Child** beside the box (or move to it with `↓` and press `Enter`; when nothing matches, `Enter` alone creates it).

- The node is created as a **child of the node you are on**, named exactly as you typed it.
- You **stay where you are**. The new node joins the trail once you visit it.
- Create Child shows even when matches are listed, and is greyed out when no node is selected.

## Back and Forward

The **←** and **→** arrows at the top left of the graph, or **Alt+←** and **Alt+→**, step through the nodes you have visited, one node per press, like a browser.

- Going to a new node after Back clears what was ahead.
- An arrow is greyed out when there is nothing on its side.
- The keys do nothing while you are typing in a field.
- Back and Forward start fresh when you open or reload a graph.
- The browser's own Back button still leaves the graph.

The **Library** button beside the arrows opens your graph library.

## The trail

The strip along the bottom edge of the graph is the **trail**: the last **20** nodes you visited in this graph.

- Oldest on the left, newest on the right, separated by `>`.
- The node you are on is the **rightmost** chip, outlined.
- Visiting a node again **moves it to the right end**; it never shows twice.
- Click any chip to go back to that node in **one click**.
- A long trail scrolls sideways.
- Each graph keeps its own trail in this browser, so it is still there after a reload. Opening a graph lands on its stem node, which moves to the right end.

## Help and Settings

**Help → FAQ** and **your avatar** (Settings) open in a panel on the right, over the page you are on, without leaving it. On a graph you can still see and pan the graph beside it.

- Close it with **×**, **Esc**, or **a click on the graph**, and you are back exactly where you were.
- Settings has a **search box** over its sections: `api` leaves only API Keys.
- A **theme** change applies the moment you pick it, with no Save, and the panel follows it.
- The FAQ is also a page at [headvroom.com/faq](https://headvroom.com/faq), for links.

## Esc, one thing at a time

Each press of `Esc` closes one thing, topmost first: the search list, then the Help/Settings panel, then the Info Panel.

## Summary

| To | Do |
|----|----|
| Find a node | Click the box or press `/`, type, `Enter` |
| Create a child here | Type the name, **Create Child** |
| Go back / forward | **←** / **→**, or **Alt+←** / **Alt+→** |
| Return to a recent node | Click its chip in the trail |
| Open the FAQ | Help → FAQ |
| Open Settings | Click your avatar |
| Close the panel | ×, Esc, or a click on the graph |
