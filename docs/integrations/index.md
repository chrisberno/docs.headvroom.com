# Integrations

Headvroom connects seamlessly with external tools and services to enhance your knowledge graph workflow.

## Available Integrations

### [MCP Server](/integrations/mcp)

Connect AI assistants like Claude, Cursor, and Windsurf directly to your knowledge graphs using the Model Context Protocol (MCP). Your AI tools can read your graphs, search across nodes, and access your recent activity—eliminating the need to re-explain context at the start of every conversation.

**Best for:** AI-powered workflows, context persistence, automated knowledge retrieval

---

### [Google Drive](/integrations/google-drive)

Link Google Drive folders to your nodes and access your documents directly within Headvroom. Browse files, preview content, and maintain connections between your knowledge graph and cloud storage.

**Best for:** Document organization, team collaboration, file reference management

---

### [Notion](/integrations/notion)

Embed published Notion pages directly in your nodes. View and edit Notion content without leaving Headvroom - changes sync in real-time.

**Best for:** Documentation, wikis, product specs, collaborative content

---

### GitHub *(Coming Soon)*

Connect repositories to your project nodes and view commits, issues, and repository data without leaving Headvroom. Keep your development context alongside your knowledge structure.

**Best for:** Development projects, code documentation, technical knowledge bases

## Integration Philosophy

Headvroom integrations follow these principles:

1. **Read-first** - Integrations start as read-only to ensure data safety
2. **Context-rich** - Bring external data into your graph's context
3. **Non-destructive** - Your source data is never modified without explicit action
4. **Authenticated** - All connections use secure, revocable credentials
